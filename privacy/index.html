Privacy policy

Focus - Timer is developed by Enric Bisbe Gil. Your focus-session history and preferences stay on your Mac; the app does not send them to the developer.

Information stored on your Mac

Focus - Timer stores preferences and focus-session history locally on your Mac. Session names may contain text you choose to enter. Basic sessions record history; the Full Version unlock makes history and statistics available in the app.

Exporting sessions creates a file in a location you choose. You decide whether to share that file with others.

Calendar integration

Calendar integration is optional and requires your permission. The app uses macOS Calendar access to show available calendars and, when enabled, add completed focus sessions to your selected calendar.

Those events may be synchronized by the calendar provider you use. That provider’s own privacy policy applies to its services. The app does not send your calendar information to the developer.

Purchases

The optional Full Version unlock is processed through Apple’s App Store and StoreKit. The app uses verified purchase transactions to determine whether Full Version is unlocked and to restore purchases. Apple processes the payment; the app does not receive your payment-card details.

Apple’s processing is governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

Notifications and automation

Notifications are delivered through macOS. Voice announcements, scripts, shortcuts, and external automation are optional features. Scripts and integrations you configure may interact with other apps or services under your control.

Developer collection and tracking

The current app does not include developer-operated analytics, advertising, or tracking services. Local preferences and session history are not sent to the developer by the app.

Support correspondence

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send, such as descriptions of a problem or screenshots. We use that information to respond to your enquiry and troubleshoot the issue. We do not use it for advertising or sell it.

The support mailbox is hosted by Proton; its handling of email is described in Proton’s privacy policy. Support correspondence is retained only as long as needed to handle the enquiry and related follow-up, or meet applicable legal obligations. You can email us to request deletion of your correspondence.

This website

This website does not add analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, or cookies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub states that it logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes; see GitHub Pages data collection and GitHub’s privacy statement for details.

Your choices

You can change app preferences, revoke macOS permissions, and decide whether to use Calendar, exports, or automation. Locally stored session information is under your control; the developer cannot access or delete it remotely. Contact support for help managing it.

Contact and updates

For privacy enquiries, contact Enric Bisbe Gil at focustimer.support@proton.me.

We will update this policy when the app’s handling of information changes and revise the effective date on this page.
