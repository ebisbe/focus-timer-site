Support

A quick guide to focusing, taking breaks, and getting help.

Getting started

Launch Focus - Timer, then click the Timer icon in your Mac’s menu bar. The app does not display a Dock icon. Use the menu to start a focus session and open Settings.

In Settings → Timer & Breaks, choose your focus-session and break lengths. Settings → Sounds controls session alerts, break alerts, and ticking sounds.

Free and Full Version

Basic focus sessions are free. An optional one-time Full Version purchase unlocks statistics/history and export, Calendar integration, voice announcements, scripts, keyboard shortcuts, external automation, and advanced timer controls. It is not a subscription.

Focus history is recorded before upgrading and becomes visible after unlocking Full Version.

Restore a purchase

Open Settings → Upgrade → Restore Purchases. Use the Apple Account that originally purchased Full Version. Check your network connection if the purchase or restore cannot be completed.

If Full Version still appears locked, email support with your app and macOS versions and any error message. Do not send payment details or your Apple Account password.

Permissions and troubleshooting

  • Notifications: enable Timer in System Settings → Notifications if alerts are not appearing. Check your macOS Focus settings too.
  • Calendar: integration is optional. Allow access when requested, or review the Calendar permission in System Settings → Privacy & Security.
  • Launch at login: configure it in Timer’s General settings and review macOS Login Items if necessary.
  • Automation: scripts and integration with other apps may require additional macOS permissions.

Requirements

macOS 13 or later.
